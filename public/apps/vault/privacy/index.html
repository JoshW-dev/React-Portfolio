Simple Vault privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Simple Vault: Encrypted Photos, called Simple Vault below, is an iPhone app made by Joshua Wade. It collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics or ads, and it doesn't connect to the internet. I can't see what you put in it, and no third party receives it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The photos, videos and files you add. Simple Vault encrypts each one with AES-256-GCM and keeps it in the app's private storage.
  • The encryption key. It stays in your iPhone's Keychain, sealed with your passcode. If you turn on Face ID, a second copy sits behind Face ID.
  • A temporary decrypted copy while a video or document is open, or while you share an item. Simple Vault deletes the copy when you close the item, when the app locks, and the next time the app opens.

Simple Vault's files are left out of iCloud and computer backups.

Permissions

  • Photos: you pick items with Apple's photo picker, which needs no permission. Simple Vault asks for full Photos access only if you choose to delete the originals after adding them. It saves to your library only when you export an item there.
  • Face ID: iOS checks your face, and Simple Vault receives only a yes or no.

You can turn these off at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Simple Vault.

Sharing

Items leave Simple Vault only when you share them. The app or person you share with then has their own copy.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've turned on Share With App Developers in iOS, Apple may send me crash reports and anonymous usage statistics. These come from iOS and don't include your files.

Deleting your data

Deleting an item in Simple Vault removes it for good. Deleting the app removes everything in it. No other copy exists, so I can't restore anything, and nobody can recover a lost passcode.

Changes and contact

I'll post any change to this policy on this page. Send questions to josh@method-ai.com.
