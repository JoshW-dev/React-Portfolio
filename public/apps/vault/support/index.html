Vault support

Email support@joshwade.site with any question about Vault.

I forgot my passcode

If Face ID is on, open Vault with Face ID, then tap the gear and choose Change Passcode. If Face ID is off, the vault can't be opened, and nobody can recover it.

I got a new iPhone

Vault's contents don't move to a new phone, because the key never leaves the old one. Before you switch, open each item, tap Share and save it somewhere safe.

I deleted the app

Deleting Vault deletes everything in it, and the items can't be restored.

Why does Vault ask for full access to Photos?

It asks only when you choose to delete originals after adding them. Deleted originals stay in the Recently Deleted album in Photos for 30 days, so delete them there to remove them sooner.

Why does a large video take a moment to open?

Vault decrypts the whole file before it plays. While the video is open, it needs free space equal to the video's size.
